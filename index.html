Questions

Before you start a crew.

Is Lograr on the App Store or Google Play?

The iPhone and Android apps are in beta. Until they're out, Lograr runs in your browser at lograr.live and installs to your home screen like an app.

Does it cost anything?

No. Crews, logging, imports and backup are free, with no ads. Pro is an optional upgrade for longer charts, muscle volume and looks.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Import a CSV from Strong, Hevy or your own spreadsheet, and your sets, PRs and gym days come with you.

Do I need an account?

Not to log your own workouts. You need an email sign-in to start or join a crew, so your crew can see when you went.

How big can a crew be?

Up to 30 people, and you can be in up to 3 crews. A crew of 4 or fewer keeps its streak when everyone hits their week. In a bigger crew, three quarters of the members need to.

Does cardio count?

Yes, cardio counts toward your weekly goal, and there are run plans. But lifting is the point, so the leaderboard and most challenges are about the barbell.

What can my crew see?

Your name, your best bench, squat and deadlift, what you lifted this week, which days you showed up, and your streaks. Never the rest of your log, and your bodyweight only if you opt in.
