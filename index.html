How it works

Three taps. Zero excuses.

  1. 1

    Start a crew

    Name it, set your gym days a week, and drop the invite link in the group chat. Up to 30 people.

  2. 2

    Log your day in one tap

    Upper, Lower or Other. Or turn on gym check-in and it logs itself when you walk in.

  3. 3

    See who went

    Everyone who lifted, everyone resting, everyone skipping. Poke whoever didn't show.

Weekly challenge

Last place wears the chicken.

Every Monday your crew gets a new challenge, picked for you so nobody argues about it. Most gym days, most deadlift volume, a rep-out at 80% of your max, a bench-off. Top score wins. Last place gets a 🐔 next to their name for the whole week.

  • A different kind every week, never the same twice in a row
  • Crew vs crew: take on another crew, most gym days per member wins
  • Badges for wins, streaks, comebacks and giant kills

Gym check-in

Walk in. It's logged.

Save your gym and turn on check-in. When you show up, the day logs itself, or it logs when Apple Health records a workout. No phone out between sets, no "forgot to log it" excuses.

  • One tap to log by hand: Upper, Lower or Other
  • Cardio counts toward your weekly goal too
  • Week streaks only break on a short week, never on a rest day

Questions

Before you start a crew.

Is Lograr on the App Store or Google Play?

Not yet. The iPhone app is in TestFlight and the Android app is in a closed test. Until they're out, Lograr runs in your browser at lograr.live and installs to your home screen, with its own icon and no browser bars.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need an account?

Not to log your own workouts. You need an email sign-in to start or join a crew, so your crew can see when you went.

How big can a crew be?

Up to 30 people, and you can be in up to 3 crews. A crew of 4 or fewer keeps its streak when everyone hits their week. In a bigger crew, three quarters of the members need to.

Does cardio count?

Yes, cardio counts toward your weekly goal. But lifting is the point, so the scoreboard and most challenges are about the barbell.

What can my crew see?

Your name, your best bench, squat and deadlift, what you lifted this week, which days you showed up, and your streaks. Never the rest of your log, and your bodyweight only if you opt in.

Bring your crew.

Open Lograr, start a crew and drop the link in your group chat. It takes about a minute.
